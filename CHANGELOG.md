# CHANGELOG.md

## [1.0.0] — 2026-11-07
### Added
- **Monte Carlo Execution & Slippage Modeling Lab**: Interactive multi-path simulator supporting Almgren-Chriss optimal execution trajectories, TWAP, VWAP, and POV schedules under Geometric Brownian Motion, Jump-Diffusion, and Heston stochastic volatility.
- **Implementation Shortfall Risk Analytics**: Full distributional histogram of execution costs (bps) with Expected Shortfall, 95%/99% Value-at-Risk (VaR), 95%/99% Conditional VaR (CVaR), and a 4-Regime Execution Stress Matrix.
- **Quantitative Factor & Backtesting Engine**: 10 canonical and physics-inspired factor families with Rank IC, IC Decay, Quantile spreads, and walk-forward transaction-cost-aware backtesting.
- **QuantPhysics Laboratory**: Interactive SDE simulations for GBM, Ornstein-Uhlenbeck, Heston Stochastic Volatility, and Langevin dynamics.
- **13-Agent Autonomous Research Observatory & Improvement Lab**: Multi-agent research workflow with 15-field Improvement Cards and before/after ablation comparisons.
- **30-Section Exhaustive Scientific Report Generator**: Automated generation and independent audit verification of institutional research reports.

## [2.5.0] — 2026-11-07

- **WorldPulse Global Financial Intelligence & Geospatial Research Engine**: Independent 3D geospatial financial intelligence application inside AlphaForge Capital featuring a Three.js interactive 3D globe, 7 geospatial telemetry layers, Historical World Replay scrubber, Plain-English + Quant Dual-View Briefing, Financial Knowledge Graph Explorer, News & Narrative Disentangler, Judge Counterfactual Recalculator, Unseen Data & Prompt-Injection Stress Suite, Risk Guardian Kill-Switch Gate, Cross-Lab Integration, and 3-Tier Report Generator.
